Multi-Objective and Multi-Task Optimization for Robust AI


February 22–23, 2027, Montréal, Canada

Call for Participation

Overview

We invite researchers, practitioners, and students to participate in the AAAI-27 Workshop on Multi-Objective and Multi-Task Optimization for Robust AI, a one-day workshop at the Forty-First AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Montréal, Canada. The workshop will take place on February 22 or 23, 2027; the exact date will be assigned by AAAI.

Trustworthy AI is inherently multi-dimensional. Modern AI systems must remain accurate and useful while also being robust to adversarial perturbations and distribution shift, safe, fair, privacy-preserving, transparent, reliable, and efficient. These requirements often interact or conflict, and a single scalar score can obscure the trade-offs that matter for training, evaluation, and deployment.

The workshop connects multi-objective optimization (MOO), evolutionary computation, multitask learning, and robust and trustworthy AI. We will explore how Pareto-aware optimization, hard constraints, preference elicitation, and knowledge transfer across tasks can address competing trustworthiness requirements in foundation models, reinforcement learning, and autonomous systems.

The workshop builds on the research agenda of the NeurIPS 2026 Self-Evolving Diversity-Driven Search for Robust AI Systems (EvoRobust) workshop, with a central focus on multi-objective and multi-task optimization. We welcome theory, algorithms, applications, and evaluations, including related work that motivates new optimization formulations. Contributions from early-career researchers and across disciplines are particularly encouraged.
